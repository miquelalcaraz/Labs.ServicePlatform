## Core business terms

- **Subscription**: contractual entitlement that grants access to the platform under a specific plan.
- **Subscription Plan**: catalog definition of features, limits and rules available for a subscription.
- **Tenant**: logical isolation boundary that groups one or more companies under the same customer space.
- **Company**: legal or operational business entity inside a tenant.
- **Owner Company**: the primary company that owns the subscription.
- **Admin User**: initial external user with the highest permissions allowed by the subscription.
- **Internal User**: QLT System employee using the backoffice or internal tools.
- **External User**: customer-side user belonging to a tenant/company.
- **Membership**: link between a user and a tenant/company, including role assignment and status.
- **Onboarding**: process that initializes a new customer setup.
- **Backoffice**: internal operational application used by QLT System staff.
- **Sales Opportunity** or **Deal**: commercial sale input that originates the onboarding.
- **Project Manager**: internal role responsible for initializing and configuring the customer account.

## Identity and access terms

- **Identity User**: authenticated subject stored in Identity.
- **Role**: authorization grouping.
- **Permission**: granular authorization capability.
- **Client Application**: application or API registered in Identity.
- **Activation**: process through which a user sets credentials and gains access.
- **Invitation**: communication sent to enable initial login.
